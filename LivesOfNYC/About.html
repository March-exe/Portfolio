About Me

Marciano Faugno

Marciano Faugno is a multidisciplinary artist working across animation, photography, film, graphic design, spray-paint, and traditional media. His work crosses between mediums frequently, exploring the relationship we have with art in space and time. He is driven by an interest in video game culture and graffiti street art. He approaches creative work through experimentation and iteration, valuing collaboration and feedback as essential parts of the creative process.
